Experience

End-to-end ownership across ML research, data engineering, and production systems.

2025 - 2026

I am a Data & ML Engineer at Purple Dental in Glastonbury, CT, where I engineered Python automation and SQL pipelines on AWS (S3, Lambda, Redshift). I consolidated multiple operational sources to cut data retrieval times from 3 hours to under 5 minutes.

My work involves architecting event-driven streaming pipelines via Apache Kafka for real-time telemetry and automating insurance claims monitoring, which successfully recovered $45K in annual revenue. I also integrated LLMs using the OpenAI API and LangChain for extracting structured clinical entities, and deployed deep learning computer vision pipelines using PyTorch for dental X-ray classification.

2024

As an Associate Data Scientist at Casey's General Stores, I designed enterprise Star Schema data models in Snowflake using dbt Core. I productionized demand forecasting models leveraging Prophet and XGBoost on Azure Databricks, which improved MAPE from 22% to 16% across over 2,900 stores.

I also built fuel pricing and inventory optimization models that increased our gross profit margin by 4.2 percentage points in pilot markets. By designing A/B testing infrastructure for 40+ concurrent experiments, I helped identify $2.3M in incremental annual revenue, further applying Difference-in-Differences causal inference to isolate causal lifts in cross-promotional purchasing.

2023 - 2024

During my tenure as a Data Science Intern at Casey's, I focused on building automated anomaly detection models for inventory monitoring. This flagged stockouts 2 to 3 days earlier and cut manual review time by 70%.

Additionally, I trained PyTorch YOLO object detection models to identify out-of-stock products directly from retail aisle images, establishing OpenCV workflows and deploying them via a FastAPI inference service for near real-time shelf compliance monitoring.

2022 - 2023

As a Graduate Research Assistant in the CALS AI & Precision Agriculture Lab at Iowa State University, I developed an LSTM and XGBoost ensemble model for crop yield prediction. By fusing satellite imagery, IoT soil sensors, and weather data, the ensemble achieved a 14% accuracy improvement over baseline models.

I also engineered Apache Airflow ETL workflows and built geospatial ML pipelines using GeoPandas and Rasterio for drone and satellite imagery processing, generating variable rate application maps that successfully reduced estimated input costs by 18%.

2018 - 2019

In the Bioinformatics & ML Lab at Vardhaman College of Engineering as an Undergraduate Research Assistant, I applied supervised learning to highly complex genomic and proteomic datasets.

I preprocessed high-dimensional RNA-seq and protein data utilizing PCA and t-SNE for dimensionality reduction, and trained cross-validated Random Forest and SVM classifiers to successfully identify gene expression signatures associated with specific disease phenotypes.
